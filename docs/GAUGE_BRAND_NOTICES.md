# Gauge brand references

RomRaider2 is an independent community project. The STI Night and Evolution Night
themes do not indicate sponsorship, approval or official vehicle compatibility.

## STI wordmark

At the project owner's request, STI Night includes the STI wordmark centered on
the dial. The reference is the inline 74 × 28 SVG wordmark on the
[Subaru Tecnica International website](https://www.sti.jp/en/), retrieved
September 6, 2026. `GaugeArtwork` converts that geometry to native vector path
commands; it does not reproduce the website's application code or a cluster photo.

STI and its wordmark belong to Subaru Tecnica International. They are not original
RomRaider2 artwork. The project's software license does not grant trademark
rights, and this provenance notice is not a permission or endorsement claim.

## Evolution Night

Evolution Night is an RR2 interpretation of Mitsubishi performance instrumentation,
not a replica of a particular cluster. Its layout and materials are drawn in code.
The theme does not display fabricated ACD modes, warning lamps or odometer data.
Mitsubishi and Evolution names remain associated with their respective owners.

## Visual-reference photographs

Cluster photos inspected during design review are not bundled with the application.
The software's scales remain reference or user-configured display ranges; no
photographed redline has been adopted as a universal engine limit.
